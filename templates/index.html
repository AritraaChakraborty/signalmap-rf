What This Tool Computes Pipeline

Every uploaded or sample CSV row is checked against physical coordinate limits (0–100 m) and plausible Wi-Fi RSSI bounds (-110 to -10 dBm). Invalid rows are separated with explicit rejection reasons rather than silently dropped.

Valid points are fitted to the indoor log-distance path-loss model RSSI(d) = A - 10·n·log10(d), then combined with inverse-distance weighting to estimate coverage across unsurveyed regions.

Core Analytical Questions Evaluation

1. Where are the weak zones? Compare room-level averages and individual dropouts in Coverage Map and Statistics.

2. Does the threshold change the verdict? Inspect strict (-70 dBm), default (-75 dBm), and lenient (-80 dBm) sensitivity plus gap confidence in Analysis.
