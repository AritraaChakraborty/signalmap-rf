About Wireless-Signal Coverage Mapper

An individual Python and web-based RF survey analysis tool designed to validate spatial RSSI measurements, model indoor path loss, compare floor zones, and communicate uncertainty over unsurveyed regions.

Course Outcome (CO1–CO6) Integration Implementation Map
Outcome Module Implemented Functionality
CO1 core/models.py Validation functions (is_valid_coordinate, is_valid_rssi), zone classification, and Euclidean distance computation.
CO2 core/io_utils.py Dictionary/list data structures, CSV reading & writing, synthetic survey generation, and row-level rejection logging.
CO3 core/models.py SurveyPoint (__init__, __repr__, __eq__, __lt__) and SurveyDataset (__init__, __len__, __getitem__, __iter__).
CO4 core/analysis_np_pd.py NumPy arrays for vectorized distances, 2D spatial grid summaries, interpolated floor surfaces, and log-distance OLS regression.
CO5 core/analysis_np_pd.py Pandas DataFrame location comparisons, weak-point percentage aggregation, and CSV/JSON summary exports.
CO6 core/plotting.py Well-labelled Matplotlib scatter map, hatched coverage heatmap, RSSI histogram, and path-loss fit plots.
Why Special Methods Matter CO3 Justification

Implementing __len__, __getitem__, and __iter__ on SurveyDataset lets the validated collection behave like a native Python sequence—supporting len(ds), slicing ds[:5], and direct iteration without exposing internal mutable lists.

Implementing __lt__ on SurveyPoint enables direct sorting and min()/max() queries by signal strength.
