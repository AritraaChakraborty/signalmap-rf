Handling Missing Locations & Limitations Methodological Caveats

Missing locations: Pure spatial averaging (IDW) fails cleanly in unsurveyed corners because it pulls values from distant rooms without accounting for radial attenuation. Blending IDW with the fitted log-distance path-loss model yields physically monotonic estimates in the empty northeast sector (x > 28 m, y > 18 m), which is explicitly hatched as low-confidence because actual partition walls inside that gap were never measured.

Limitations: (1) Built-in records are synthetically generated, not collected from live campus hardware; (2) the isotropic log-distance model ignores directional antenna patterns and exact wall material geometry; (3) single-snapshot RSSI does not capture co-channel interference or temporal fading.
